A Gentle Space

No feeds, no ads, no streaks. Set the text size and pick a page colour, from white to night. The apps add typefaces, OpenDyslexic among them.

Yours to Keep

Your books stay on your device and open without a connection. Books and Comics have no account to sign in to, and we keep no copy of your library.

Want your place on every device? Armarium syncs it with the Books apps (coming soon) and with Comics.

Open Catalogues

Bring DRM-free files from your own folders, or browse OPDS catalogues and download straight to your shelf. Comics also reads Kavita libraries.

Your Own Library Server

Armarium is a small server for the books and comics you already have, in plain folders or Calibre libraries. Run it at home and pair Skrivist with a QR code.

  • Free and open source
  • No ads, no tracking
  • Your books and reading progress, synced with Skrivist Books for iPhone, iPad and Android (coming soon)
  • Your comics and reading progress, streamed and synced in Skrivist Comics' Armarium mode
