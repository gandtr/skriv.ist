Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gand Technology Services ("Gand", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the skriv.ist website and the Skrivist readers (together, the "Service"): Skrivist Comics (comics.skriv.ist and the desktop app) and Skrivist Books (books.skriv.ist, the desktop app, and the iOS and Android apps). Skrivist Cloud, our hosted reader at app.skriv.ist, closed on October 24, 2026, and its data was deleted the same day (section 1). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

Skrivist Comics and Skrivist Books

Comics and Books have no Skrivist accounts and no cloud library. Imported files, reading progress, notes, and settings stay on your device, apart from what goes to a library server you connect and your device's own backups (both below); we do not receive them. The web readers keep this data in browser storage. The macOS and Windows desktop readers and the iOS and Android apps use local app storage, with a private database and copies of imported and downloaded files. Importing does not move or delete your original files.

If you connect an OPDS catalogue or a library server, the reader talks directly to the server you choose. Its address and any sign-in details you choose to save are stored on your device; the iOS app keeps passwords and access tokens in the iOS Keychain, and the Android app keeps them encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore. A reader paired with an Armarium library server also sends it your reading position and read or unread status for that server's books, so another device can continue where you left off. That server's operator handles your requests under its own privacy practices.

The desktop readers contain no analytics or tracking SDKs and do not send us usage or diagnostic data. Our websites count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or identify individuals. Links from the desktop app open in your system browser; the destination website's privacy practices apply when you visit it.

The iOS and Android apps contain no advertising or tracking SDKs, add no analytics of their own, and send us nothing. They use the camera only to scan the QR code that pairs a library server; the image is processed on the device and is not stored. On Android, that scanner is Google's code scanner from Google Play services: Google receives diagnostic and usage information from it (device model and OS version, app package and version, device or per-installation identifiers, and performance and error data) under Google's privacy policy, and does not share it with third parties. Downloads from your library servers use the system's background download services.

Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on iOS, backup to your Google account on Android) can include the apps' library database: the list of your books, reading positions, notes, and settings, under Apple's or Google's terms. Book files and covers are left out of these backups, and saved passwords and tokens never leave the device.

Skrivist Cloud (closed)

Skrivist Cloud was our hosted reader at app.skriv.ist, with its own API and hosted text-to-speech voices. It stopped on October 24, 2026. app.skriv.ist now redirects to skriv.ist, and the API and the hosted voices no longer answer.

While it ran, you signed in to Skrivist Cloud through vigil.center, Gand's single sign-on service, with your Google account (or, for some accounts, an email address and password). We received a user identifier and your display name, not your email address. For each account we stored the EPUB files you uploaded, covers, book metadata, reading progress and per-book settings, and our servers kept operational logs.

On October 24, 2026 we deleted the servers, databases and storage that ran Skrivist Cloud. All Skrivist Cloud accounts, uploaded books, reading progress, API keys and server logs went with them. Nothing was kept or exported. Google Cloud erases deleted projects for good within 30 days. Books the app cached in your browser stay there until you clear the browser's site data for app.skriv.ist.

Local device data

Theme and language preferences on our websites are stored in your browser's localStorage.

Usage and diagnostic data

The Service contains no advertising or behavioral-analytics trackers. Our websites use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or identify individuals.

  • To diagnose issues and improve the Service

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted service providers who help us operate the Service:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, analytics, and security

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or to prevent harm.

4. Data retention

Skrivist Cloud data was deleted on October 24, 2026, as described in section 1.

Data kept by Skrivist Comics and Skrivist Books stays on your device (and in the places described in section 1). For the web readers, clearing the site's data removes it. For the desktop readers and the iOS and Android apps, deleting imported books or removing the app's local data removes the corresponding local copies. This does not remove your original files or copies on a library server. Uninstalling the iOS or Android app removes its data from the device, with two exceptions: a backup of the device may still hold the library database (see section 1), and iOS may keep a library server's saved password or token in the Keychain; remove the library in Settings before uninstalling to delete it. Uninstalling a desktop app may leave its local data behind; contact us for help resetting an installation.

5. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Skrivist Cloud accounts were deleted on October 24, 2026, so there is nothing left to request for them. You may also manage cookies and local storage through your browser settings.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including on servers operated by Cloudflare. We rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect your data during international transfers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact us
